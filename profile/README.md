## What we do

The Rose-Barrie Foundation researches health-monitoring and related technologies and gives the results away.

Designs, schematics, layouts, firmware, documentation and test data are published to public repositories under open licences. Anyone can read them, build from them, improve them, or manufacture from them. There is no paywall, no membership, no waiting list, and no early access for anyone.

We are a non-profit society incorporated in British Columbia, Canada.

## Why

Health-monitoring hardware is expensive, closed, and slow to reach the people who need it most. Designs sit behind patents. Firmware can't be audited. Data goes somewhere you can't see.

Open designs can be built and repaired by anyone, audited by anyone, and can't be withdrawn once published. That's the wager this project is making.

## How we publish

Our [Publication Policy](https://rosebarrie.org) is public, and it binds us:

- Everything released is released **openly, under an open licence, at no cost**
- Everything is released **simultaneously to the public**
- **No advance access** for anyone — no partner, no sponsor, no connected company
- **No private distribution channel** exists

We publish in a way intended to establish our work as prior art, so that nobody can patent what we've disclosed. We do not file patents on work we publish.

## Privacy

**We collect what the law requires us to collect, and we tell you what that is. Everything else is your choice.**

You decide what to share, and you can change your mind at any time. Nothing is on by default.

In our projects and devices:

- **Nothing is collected without your express consent** — no telemetry by default, no pre-ticked boxes, no consent bundled into anything else
- Consent is **granular** — separate for each purpose — and **reversible**
- Declining costs you nothing. No feature depends on data collection

**Identifiable personal data will never be transmitted to, or held by, the Foundation.** We will not publish a design that requires it.

What we publish is research, designs, methods and test results.

These commitments are written into our bylaws, which bar the Foundation from collecting or holding identifiable personal data at all — except under a research protocol approved in advance by an independent ethics board, with each participant's express consent. Changing that requires a three-quarters majority of our voting members.

## Use of AI

We use AI tools across this project — drafting, code, and design work. Most organisations building anything similar do, and we would rather say so than leave you guessing.

**No personal data goes into them.** The Foundation holds no identifiable personal data, so there is none to put in.

If that ever changes — a future research study, approved in advance by an independent ethics board — any AI used on that data would run **locally, on our own infrastructure**, never a third-party service. We would name the model, and consent would say so specifically. It would never be used to make a decision about a person.

- Every decision, review and merge is made by a person
- The commit history is the record — you can see what was built and when
- Where a device of ours uses machine learning, it runs **on the device**. Nothing is transmitted for it, and we will say plainly what it does and does not infer

Our full [AI Use Statement](https://rosebarrie.org) sets this out in detail.

## Contributing

Contributions are welcome.

Before we can accept a substantial contribution, we ask you to sign a [Contributor Licence Agreement](https://rosebarrie.org). In short:

- **You keep the copyright** in everything you write
- You grant us the right to publish it, and to sell commercial licences to companies that want to build closed products on this work
- **We commit to keep publishing your contribution openly.** If we ever stop, that commercial right comes back to you
- The Foundation is a non-profit society. It cannot distribute income to its members, and anything left on dissolution must go to another non-profit
- **No agreement is needed** for typo fixes, formatting, or changes under 15 lines

The reasoning is set out in full in [CONTRIBUTING.md](#), including why we ask for this at all.

## Supporting the work

> **We are not a registered charity.** We cannot issue tax receipts, and gifts to the Foundation are **not tax-deductible**.

We accept support from people who want the work to exist. We decline any gift conditional on withholding publication, on advance access, on a particular research conclusion, or on an endorsement. Our [Gift Acceptance Policy](https://rosebarrie.org) sets out the full list.

Recognition is not influence. Supporters are not members, hold no vote, have no board seat, and receive nothing ahead of the public.

## Commercial licensing

Our work is published under strongly reciprocal licences, which means anything built on it must also be published openly.

If you need different terms — to build a closed product on our designs — get in touch. We are not a registered charity and receive no grant funding, so commercial licensing is one of the few ways this research gets paid for.

We haven't set terms yet. When we do, they will be published.
