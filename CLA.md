# Contributor Licence Agreement

**The Rose-Barrie Foundation** · Individual Contributor Licence Agreement · Version 1.0

---

Thank you for contributing. This agreement lets the Foundation use and publish your contribution, and lets it fund its research by offering commercial licences.

**You keep the copyright in everything you write.** If the Foundation ever stops publishing openly, the commercial rights you granted come back to you.

To sign, comment on your pull request with exactly:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

## 1. Definitions

**"Foundation"** means The Rose-Barrie Foundation, a society incorporated under the *Societies Act* (British Columbia).

**"Contribution"** means any work of authorship, including any modification or addition to existing work, that You submit to the Foundation for inclusion in any of its projects. Submission includes any form of electronic or written communication sent to the Foundation or its representatives, including a pull request, patch, issue attachment or mailing list post, but excludes anything conspicuously marked "Not a Contribution."

**"Open Licence"** means a licence that permits any person to use, study, modify and redistribute the licensed work, including the CERN Open Hardware Licence v2, the GNU General Public License v3.0, and Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0.

**"You"** means the individual agreeing to this document.

## 2. You keep your copyright

You retain all right, title and interest in and to Your Contributions. This agreement grants licences. It does not assign or transfer ownership of anything.

## 3. Copyright licence

You grant to the Foundation a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable (except as provided in section 7) licence to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform, sublicense and distribute Your Contributions and such derivative works.

The right to sublicense includes the right to grant licences on terms other than an Open Licence, so that the Foundation may fund its research through commercial licensing under its Bylaws.

## 4. Patent licence

You grant to the Foundation and to recipients of work distributed by the Foundation a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable patent licence to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import and otherwise transfer Your Contribution, where such licence applies only to those patent claims licensable by You that are necessarily infringed by Your Contribution alone or by combination of Your Contribution with the project to which it was submitted.

If any entity institutes patent litigation alleging that Your Contribution, or the work to which You contributed, constitutes direct or contributory patent infringement, any patent licence granted under this section to that entity terminates as of the date such litigation is filed.

## 5. The Foundation's commitment to you

The Foundation undertakes that every Contribution it accepts will be published and remain available under an Open Licence.

The Foundation may in addition grant commercial licences on other terms. It may not use the rights granted under this agreement to cease publishing Your Contribution under an Open Licence.

*This commitment reflects sections 6.2 and 9.1 of the Foundation's Bylaws, which are entrenched and may not be altered except by the higher threshold in section 10.2.*

## 6. What you confirm

(a) each Contribution is Your original creation, or You have the right to submit it under this agreement;

(b) if Your employer has rights in Your Contribution, You have permission to make the Contribution on their behalf, or Your employer has waived those rights;

(c) You will identify any third-party material in Your Contribution and any licence or restriction attached to it, of which You are personally aware;

(d) You are not aware of any patent claim, other than those You license under section 4, that would be infringed by Your Contribution; and

(e) where You used an AI tool in preparing Your Contribution, You directed and shaped the result rather than simply submitting the tool's output, and You have the rights You are granting under sections 3 and 4.

*You are not required to disclose which tools You used.*

Contributions are provided on an "as is" basis. You are not expected to provide support, and unless required by law You provide no warranties or conditions of any kind.

## 7. Reversion

**7.1 Trigger.** If the Foundation ceases to make Your Contribution available under an Open Licence for a continuous period of twelve (12) months, You may give the Foundation written notice specifying the failure.

**7.2 Cure.** The Foundation has ninety (90) days from receiving the notice to resume making Your Contribution available under an Open Licence.

**7.3 Effect.** If the Foundation does not cure within that period, the right to sublicense Your Contribution on terms other than an Open Licence, granted in section 3, terminates. All other rights granted under this agreement continue, including the patent licence in section 4.

**7.4 Third parties are not affected.** Any licence already granted by the Foundation to a third party before termination under section 7.3 continues in full force. Reversion is prospective only and does not disturb any licence already granted, whether an Open Licence or a commercial licence.

**7.5 Scope.** Reversion under this section affects only Your Contributions. It does not transfer the Foundation's projects, its other rights, or any stewardship role, and it does not affect contributions made by any other person.

## 8. De minimis contributions

This agreement is not required for a Contribution that consists solely of a correction of a typographical error, a formatting change, or a change of fewer than fifteen (15) lines that does not add new functionality or design. Such Contributions are accepted under the project's Open Licence.
